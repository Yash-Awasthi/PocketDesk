PocketDesk: what is left

Scope as agreed: Windows PC + Android phone first. The PC browser page is the phone only (Phone
screen and Phone storage tabs). No outside programs (no tmux, WSL, scrcpy or adb on the user's side).

Known limits (nothing to fix)
- Android ends a screen share when the phone locks; Swap asks again after unlocking.
- Apps that mark their content sensitive (Gmail, Chrome pages) show black in any screen share.
- PC control needs "PocketDesk PC control" on in Accessibility (sideloaded installs: App info >
  Allow restricted settings first); under Android 17 Advanced Protection it stays view only.
- Phone storage needs "All files access" for PocketDesk and covers shared storage only.
- Copilot, Cline, Roo, Cursor and Antigravity conversations are read-only outside their editor;
  VS Code extension chat panels have no external API.
- Antigravity transcripts are grouped by search-index chunk, not by step.
- `pocketdesk read` rebuilds the screen from scrollback on each call; wide CJK/emoji cells shift a line.
